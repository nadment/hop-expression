== Date and time formats

[cols="<2,^1,<5", options="header"]
|===
|Input|Case sensitive|Description
|- / , . ; : = ( ) "text"||Always preserved as-is.
|A.D. AD B.C. BC|&#10003;|Era designator, with or without periods
|A.M. AM P.M. PM|&#10003;|Ante Meridiem (am) / Post Meridiem (pm) for use with HH12
|CC SCC||Century (output only)
|D||Day of week (output only)
|DAY|&#10003;|Name of day (output only)
|DY|&#10003;|Abbreviated day name (output only)
|DD||Day of month (01-31)
|DDD||Day in the year (001-366)
|DL||Long date format (output only)
|DS||Short date format (output only)
|E|&#10003;|Abbreviated era name (Japanese, Chinese, Thai)
|EE|&#10003;|Full era name (Japanese, Chinese, Thai)
|FF FF0 FF3 FF6 FF9||Fractional seconds. FF0 (seconds), FF3 (milliseconds), FF6 (microseconds), FF9 (nanoseconds). Specifying FF is equivalent to FF6
|FM||Returns values with no leading or trailing spaces
|FX||Requires exact matches between character data and format model
|HH HH12||Hour in AM/PM (1-12)
|HH24||Hour in day (0-23)
|IW||Week of year (01-52 or 01-53) based on the ISO-8601 standard (output only)
|WW||Week of year (01-53) where week 1 starts on the first day of the year (output only)
|W||Week in month (output only)
|IYYY IYY IY I||Last 4/3/2/1 digit(s) of ISO year (output only)
|RRRR RR||Last 4/2 digits of year
|Y,YYY||Year with comma
|YEAR SYEAR|&#10003;|Year spelled out (S prefixes BC years with minus sign) (output only)
|YYYY SYYYY||4-digit year (S prefixes BC years with minus sign)
|YYY YY Y||Last 3/2/1 digit(s) of year
|J||Julian day (number of days since January 1, 4712 BC)
|MI||Minutes (00-59)
|MM||Month in year (01-12)
|MON|&#10003;|Abbreviated name of month (JAN-DEC (ENG) or JAN-DEZ (DEU))
|MONTH|&#10003;|Name of month, padded with spaces
|RM|&#10003;|Roman numeral month
|Q||Quarter of year (1-4) (output only)
|SS||Seconds (00-59)
|SSSSS||Seconds in day (output only)
|TS||Short time format (output only)
|TZD||Daylight savings time zone abbreviation
|TZR||Time zone region
|TZH||Time zone hour. Can be prefixed by +/- for sign.
|TZM||Time zone minute
|X||Local radix character
|===

* The case sensitive elements only apply to output: the case of the element sets the case of the text, for example `MON` returns `DEC` and `Mon` returns `Dec`. Input is matched regardless of case.
* Elements marked as output only are not accepted to parse a string into a date.
* Any other character raises an error.

=== Date format examples

[cols="2,2"]
|===
|Dy, DD Mon YYYY HH12:MI:SS AM|Tue, 21 Dec 2021 04:01:07 PM
|DY, DD MON YYYY HH12:MI:SS AM|TUE, 21 DEC 2021 04:01:07 PM
|YYYY-MM-DD"T"HH24:MI:SS.FF6TZH:TZM|2021-12-21T16:01:07.123456+01:00
|DD/MM/YYYY HH24:MI TZR|21/12/2021 16:01 Europe/Paris
|===
